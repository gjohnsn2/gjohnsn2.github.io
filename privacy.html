Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-25

Grant is a private tool that organizes the Gmail inboxes of one household. Only the owners of those mailboxes use it.

What it accesses

With the mailbox owner's permission, Grant reads message headers (sender, subject, date) and, when needed to sort a message, its content. It adds labels, creates Gmail filters, and sends unsubscribe requests the owner has approved. It never deletes mail.

Where the data goes

Data stays on the owner's own computer. Sender and subject lines may be processed by the owner's AI assistant to suggest labels. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google user data

Grant's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

A mailbox owner can revoke Grant's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
